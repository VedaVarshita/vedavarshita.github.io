I am a Machine Learning Engineer driven by the belief that data is the key to unlocking profound insights in an increasingly complex world. I find my inspiration at the intersection of extensive data and advanced computing, where the synergy of information and algorithms creates systems that don't just complement human cognition, but exceed it. My work is centered on the conviction that the fusion of computational prowess and rigorous mathematical frameworks is the most powerful tool we have for informed decision-making and error minimization. I don’t just build models; I architect intelligent systems designed to uncover the patterns invisible to the naked eye.

My journey into the heart of AI began in the industrial landscape at Precisely, where I specialized in "Data at Scale." As an Associate Software Engineer, I learned that the most sophisticated models are only as effective as the data pipelines supporting them. I spent my time productionizing global location intelligence pipelines and building automated QA frameworks that processed millions of records. This phase of my career was defined by precision and scalability—it taught me how to move code from a local environment into a global production system while cutting processing times by over 60%.

Driven by a desire to understand the "why" behind the algorithms I was deploying, I transitioned to Drexel University to pursue my Master’s in AI and Machine Learning. Maintaining a 3.9 CGPA, I moved beyond the application layer to explore the mathematical foundations of deep learning and cloud computing. This academic pivot allowed me to stop viewing machine learning as a "black box" and start seeing it as a landscape of probability and optimization. I became captivated by the synergy of extensive data and computational power, realizing that intelligent systems have the potential to not just mimic human cognition, but to refine it by uncovering patterns invisible to the naked eye.

Currently, I serve as an AI/ML Research Assistant at the CODED Lab, where my industrial engineering background meets abstract theoretical research. My work focuses on the cutting edge of model optimization, from developing Bayesian-inspired neural network initializations that drastically improve "warm-start" accuracy to fine-tuning 1.5B parameter Language Models using DPO and RLHF. By bridging the gap between rigorous experimental design and scalable software engineering, I am dedicated to building intelligent systems that are as mathematically sound as they are production-ready.
